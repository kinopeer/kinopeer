# The coordinator on autopilot

What changes while a task is on autopilot. Nothing here applies to a task that isn't; which tasks are is `CLAUDE.md` → Workflow → Autopilot.

- Merging: a PR of the task merges through auto-merge once the deputy approves it; the coordinator doesn't merge it by hand. A Dependabot PR the deputy approved, when the task is to bring them in, the coordinator squash-merges itself at the head the deputy approved, with `gh pr merge --squash --match-head-commit <sha>` (in a cloud session, the REST call `docs/TESTING.md` → Reviewing a PR's tests gives). After a merge the coordinator watches the runs it starts on `main` (the Edge run, a worker's deploy) and deals with a failure as with any other.
- Deciding: where `CLAUDE.md` and the docs it links tell a session to stop and ask the owner during the work (a task that grew, a check on the test stand), the coordinator decides and writes what it decided and why in a comment on the PR, naming what it didn't or couldn't check by hand; a session it started messages the coordinator instead. A product choice inside the task is the coordinator's too, recorded in `docs/SPEC.md`'s decision log, and so are the deputy's **For the coordinator** questions (the `deputy` skill → The round).
- What stays with the owner, decided as without autopilot, is only a release (its PR, its tag and the store PR) and communication with other people: an outside contributor's PR (the `deputy` skill → The owner's approval) and comments, and posting anywhere beyond the project's own PRs and issues: an idea a rule sends to Discussions → Ideas (a heuristic's rare case among them, `CLAUDE.md` → Conventions) goes into the PR's description, which SPEC's row links in the idea's place, and the coordinator's final report, for the owner to post; the PR's link stands as the record until then, and the session that next edits the row puts the idea's link in once it's posted (#575, where the two rules met). Everything else the coordinator decides and records with its reason: a change to the rules or the checks, a product decision, a visible change, credentials (a secret set, changed or removed, and a workflow that uses one).
